Transformations

The NC program is always square: X, Y and Z are at right angles to each other. Some machines still move differently – then the control converts. Here you enter which conversion your control does. So far there is one: the inclined axis. If your machine needs none, the list stays empty.

The inclined axis

On many slant-bed lathes with a Y axis, the Y slide does not move at right angles to the X slide but at an angle to it. For a Y in the program, both slides then move: Y1 moves at its angle, and X1 compensates so the tool travels exactly at right angles to X. A pure X moves only X1.

A bed that itself lies slanted in space, on the other hand, is not a transformation: the X of the program simply points along the X slide.

How the control converts

α is the angle by which the inclined axis is tilted out of the right angle to the compensating one; 0° means square.

Y1 = Y / cos α
X1 = X − Y · tan α

Example with α = 30°: for Y +10 mm, Y1 moves 11.5 mm and X1 moves back 5.8 mm.

What you enter

  • Inclined axis – the slide that moves at an angle (usually Y1).
  • Compensated by – the slide that moves along (usually X1).
  • Names in the program – what the two are called in the NC program (usually Y and X). The suggestion is the NC name without the digit.

The addon measures the angle at the joints of the assembly – the way you built the Y slide. The picture shows how it counts: positive when the inclined axis leans towards the plus side of the compensating one. Below it, an example shows how far both slides move for a Y.

Entering the angle: if you enter a different angle – for example the one from the control –, the addon turns the guideway of the inclined slide in the assembly. The slide and everything on it stay where they are; the turret stays straight, only the direction of travel changes. −89° to 89° are allowed. Cancel or Undo turns the guideway back.

Moving as in the program

In Move machine you then first move as in the program: X and Y square, and for Y both slides move. If the mouse rests on the entry here, the machine moves a Y back and forth once.

Where is the angle in the control?

  • Siemens SINUMERIK: the “inclined axis” transformation, TRAANG. The angle is in the machine data of the transformation (TRAANG_ANGLE_1).
  • Fanuc: the “Angular Axis Control” function. Angle and axes are in its parameters (parameter manual).

If your control counts the angle the other way round, it shows the same amount with the opposite sign – compare the picture with the drawing in the manual.

Handing over to CAM

FreeCAD's CAM machine definition does not know inclined axes. Since the toolpaths are square anyway, CAM gets the inclined axis the way the program sees it: square to the compensating one, with converted limits (· cos α) and the rapid rate both slides manage together. The report adds: these limits only hold while the compensating axis has room to compensate – how far it really goes is shown by “Move machine” as in the program.

What does not change

The postprocessor stays as it is: the program stays square, the control does the converting.
