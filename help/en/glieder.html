Links

A link is all bodies that are rigidly connected and therefore move together. The add-on builds them from the assembly: everything connected by a fixed joint or placed in a common sub-assembly forms one link. The grounded link is the bed – it stands still.

Example: trunnion table

The cradle of a 5-axis machine consists of a base and two arms – three bodies. If the arms are attached to the cradle with fixed joints, they swivel along as one link.

One axis, one joint

If the cradle sits in two bearing blocks, only one of them gets the revolute joint. The second bearing block is simply fixed to the bed. The assembly cannot solve two revolute joints on the same axis – the parts jump. The add-on reports such a second joint.

A body is missing?

If a body does not show up with the link it belongs to, it is not attached to any joint – it would stay behind when the machine moves. Connect it with a fixed joint. The notes below name such bodies.
