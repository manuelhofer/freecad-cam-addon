Machining (milling)

For a part that lies in a block on a mill – the tool axis is vertical. The button “Machining (milling)” opens the window; the way from the part to the operation has three steps, all in one window. For a round stock with a rotary axis there is 4-axis machining.

1. Part and stock

  • Click a face of the part (before or in the window) – best the face that should become flat. The part is chosen with it, and the job with the stock is created at once: a block around the part with an allowance on top, at the sides and below (empty: 1 mm, like FreeCAD's job). The fields change the stock right away. “Cancel” takes everything back.
  • When changing (double-click on the operation) the stock stays as it is in the job.

2. Faces

  • Without a choice the top of the part is milled flat – the highest flat faces. The sentence below the list names them.
  • A click on a face of the part adds it (another click, or a double-click in the list, removes it); green means flat facing up – it is milled flat –, red means not flat facing up, which face milling cannot do. The 3D view colours them so. “Top” selects the highest faces, “Clear selection” removes all.

3. Face milling

  • Material – from the stock, otherwise the last used; the cutting data follow it. “Tool manager …” opens it; saving there makes the window read the cutters again.
  • Cutter – a cutter with a flat tip (flat, torus or face mill) from the tool manager with cutting data for the material; Application – “Facing” preselected; speed and feed in grey below.
  • Stepdown per layer (ap), row spacing (ae, at most as wide as the flat part of the tip) and allowance – empty, the grey suggestions from the application apply.
  • The path: rows in the longer direction of the face, back and forth, in layers from the top of the stock down to the face plus allowance; along the rows the cutter centre runs 0.6 × diameter past the face, at the sides the tip reaches 0.2 × diameter past the edge – so no burr remains. Between the rows it runs semicircles (G2/G3) where both rows are free there, otherwise across at depth. Before walls and steps that stand higher the row stops (calculated against the part without the face). Rows with no stock below them are dropped. Entry is straight down with the plunge feed in the air, a ramp in material. Leaving the stock the row runs at half feed – against the burr.
  • In grey below: “→ 3 layers, 30 rows, about 2 min”. In red stands why it does not work (no cutter with cutting data, no flat face facing up, the stock does not reach above the face).
  • Create adds the tool controller and the operation “Face milling T1” to the job – one undo step (job and stock a second one). The operation has FreeCAD's depths and heights: the start depth follows the stock, the final depth is the face plus allowance, safe and clearance height come from the job's setup sheet.

Changing it later

Double-click “Face milling …” in the tree – or right-click, Edit; or select the operation or its job and press the “Machining (milling)” button – to open the window with faces, cutter, application and values of the operation. Apply changes it – one undo step; another cutter gets its own tool controller, the old one is removed if no operation uses it any more.
