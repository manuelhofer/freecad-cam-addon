New machine

New machine … builds a ready-made machine in a new document: an assembly with joints, axes with names and characteristics, spindles, tool and workpiece mounts. Afterwards Edit machine opens, where everything can be looked at and changed.

Types

You can choose a lathe with Y axis and turret, a 3-axis mill and three 5-axis mills (table/table, head/head, head/table). For the lathe and the 3-axis mill you enter the main dimensions yourself; the 5-axis mills have fixed dimensions – for trying things out.

On the table of the 3-axis mill there are two clamps as an example – the collision check (“Check on machine”) takes them into account. On your machine they are somewhere else: move or delete them in the tree.

The dimensions of the lathe

  • Name – for machine and document. Empty: “Lathe with Y axis”.
  • Bed slant – how steeply the bed rises towards the back: 0° is a flat bed, 30°, 45° or 60° are common. X travels on the bed, so it is slanted the same way.
  • Y inclined by – 0° means the Y slide moves at right angles to X. If the Y slide of your machine is built at an angle to the X slide, enter the angle here. The addon then adds the inclined axis right away: for a Y in the program both slides move, the way the control converts (see the help “Transformations”).
  • Travel X, Y, Z – from … to, counted from the position in which the machine is built; 0 lies in between. These become the limits of the joints.
  • Turret stations – the addon distributes this many stations in a circle on the face of the turret disc (P1 … Pn), each a VDI30 mount on the tool drive S3, with a block on the outside of the disc. The turret carries no fixed holders: how a tool stands is up to its holder in the tool manager – “VDI30 driven radial” puts it radial to the spindle axis, a straight one along Z.
  • Maximum speed – of the main spindle.

The dimensions of the 3-axis mill

  • Name – for machine and document. Empty: “3-axis mill”.
  • Travel X, Y, Z – from … to, as for the lathe: counted from the position in which the machine is built. These become the limits of the joints – “Check on machine” works with them.
  • Maximum speed – of spindle S1.

When you switch the type, its example values are back in the fields.

The bodies are rough, the way a machine is built in the addon. Whatever does not fit – the size of a slide, the position of the turret – you change afterwards in the assembly.

The joints are hidden – their markers (white discs with axes) covered the machine. To show them: select “Joints” under the assembly in the tree and press the space bar.
