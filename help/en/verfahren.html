Move machine

One slider per axis: drag it or type a value, and the machine in the 3D view moves along – so you see whether the axes run the right way round, what moves with what, and where it gets tight. This is the basis for the simulation.

The rows

  • Name – the NC name of the mode (e.g. X1, C4, T). Joints without a mode are shown with their name in the assembly – their position is also set by hand here. A spindle is listed under its positioning name (C4 instead of S4): turning here means positioning.
  • Slider and number field – mm for linear, degrees for rotary axes. Counted as in the assembly's joint: the position of the joint's second side relative to its first. That way the numbers match the limits.
  • Limits – from the joint's limits (minimum/maximum). It doesn't go further. If it says “no limit”, one can be entered in the joint.

What moves along

Everything behind the axis: its link and everything further out. When X1 moves, the turret moves along; when the cradle A tilts, the rotary table C tilts with it – and still stays at its angle.

OK, Cancel, Home position

  • OK keeps the position. One Ctrl+Z moves everything back.
  • Cancel moves everything back to where it was when the window opened.
  • Home position does the same without closing the window.

The position also stays when FreeCAD recomputes the assembly – the joints keep it.
