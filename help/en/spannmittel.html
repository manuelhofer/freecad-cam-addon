Workholding

The Workholding window (toolbar “CAM-Addon”, next to “Holders and tapers”) has two pages: the collection of the workshop's workholding and the setup of the job – how a job's stock sits on the table. OK takes both over, Cancel discards them.

The collection

On the left the workholding by kind: vise, self-centring vise, three-jaw chuck, clamps, zero-point system, clamping plate. Catalogue … shows the manufacturers' series with their sizes – tick what the workshop has, then Take over. New creates one with example dimensions. On the right the dimensions of the kind and the picture: from the side and from above, with an example stock in the jaws. Below the dimensions it says where they come from; anything the catalogue does not give is an example value.

  • Height – from the table up: for a vise to the top of the jaws, for a chuck the body without jaws, for plate and zero-point system the height the part sits on.
  • Clamps from/up to – the narrowest and widest stock across the jaws (for a chuck the Ø).
  • Clamping depth (usual) – how much of the stock sits in the jaws; only a few mm for 5-axis vises with gripping teeth.

The setup of the job

Four entries, as made when setting up the machine:

  1. Workholding – what the stock is clamped in. “none”: as before.
  2. Underneath – directly on the table or on a zero-point system or plate; it raises the workholding by its height.
  3. Position on the table – X and Y from the machine's workpiece mount (usually the table centre). For a vise the middle of the fixed jaw counts (it stays put when a wider part comes), otherwise the centre.
  4. Fixed jaw (vise) – back (towards the machine, default), front (towards the operator), left or right; for the others Clamps in Y or X. In the top view the operator is at the bottom.
  5. Clamping depth – empty: the workholding's usual one.

From this: the jaws touch the stock. Below it says where the job's work offset then is on the table – with OK this also applies in “Check on the machine”. The collision check there uses this workholding, the “clamped from below” check uses the clamping depth, and the setup sheet names workholding, base, position and clamping depth.
